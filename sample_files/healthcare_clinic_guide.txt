# MetroHealth Clinic & Diagnostic Center — Patient Care & Services Guide

## 1. Appointments & Consultations
- **Booking Options:** Appointments can be booked 24/7 through our online portal, phone line, or via walk-in registration at the front desk.
- **Cancellation & Rescheduling:** Please provide at least 4 hours advance notice to cancel or reschedule without incurring a missed consultation fee ($25).
- **Teleconsultation:** Available for routine follow-ups and general medical reviews between 9:00 AM and 6:00 PM Monday through Friday.

## 2. Insurance & Billing Policies
- **Accepted In-Network Providers:** BlueCross BlueShield, Aetna, Cigna, UnitedHealthcare, Medicare, and Humana.
- **Co-Pays & Deductibles:** Specialist consultations require standard co-pay collected at check-in. In-house lab tests (blood work, urinalysis) are billed directly to insurance.
- **Self-Pay / Uninsured Patients:** We offer a 20% prompt-pay discount on all outpatient physician visits and standard lab packages for patients without active insurance.

## 3. Prescription Refills & Pharmacy
- **Refill Requests:** Routine prescription refills require 24 to 48 business hours for physician review and transmission to your designated pharmacy.
- **Controlled Substances:** Prescription renewals for controlled substances (Schedule II-IV) strictly require an in-person physical evaluation every 90 days.

## 4. Diagnostic Lab & Radiology Services
- **Fasting Instructions:** Complete lipid panels and fasting blood glucose tests require 10 to 12 hours of water-only fasting prior to sample collection.
- **Test Result Delivery:** Standard blood work results are posted to the secure Patient Health Portal within 24 hours. Pathology and imaging reports (X-ray, Ultrasound) are available within 48 to 72 hours.

## 5. Emergency & Urgent Care Protocol
- **Life-Threatening Symptoms:** For chest pain, severe shortness of breath, sudden numbness, or heavy bleeding, call 911 immediately or proceed to the nearest Emergency Room.
- **Urgent Care Clinic:** MetroHealth Urgent Care operates 7 days a week from 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM for non-life-threatening conditions (minor fractures, cuts requiring stitches, high fever, seasonal flu).
