# TrendyStore — Official Customer Policies & Help Center Guide

## 1. Shipping & Delivery
- **Standard Shipping:** Delivers within 3 to 5 business days across all metropolitan areas. Free on orders above $50; otherwise a flat $4.99 fee applies.
- **Express Shipping:** Next-day delivery available for orders placed before 2:00 PM EST for a flat fee of $12.99.
- **International Shipping:** Available to over 45 countries, typically arriving within 7 to 14 business days via DHL Express.
- **Tracking:** Once dispatched, a tracking ID is sent via email and SMS within 2 hours.

## 2. Returns, Refunds & Exchanges
- **Return Window:** Customers can initiate a return or exchange within 30 days of delivery date.
- **Condition of Items:** Items must be unworn, unwashed, with all original tags and packaging intact.
- **Non-Returnable Items:** Final sale items, personalized/engraved products, and gift cards cannot be returned.
- **Refund Timeline:** Once our fulfillment center receives and inspects the return, the refund is processed to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.
- **Return Shipping Fee:** Returns are 100% free if you opt for store credit or product exchange. A $3.50 restocking fee is deducted if requesting a refund to original credit card.

## 3. Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Orders
- If an item arrives damaged or you received the wrong item, please report it within 48 hours of delivery.
- Our team will issue an immediate priority replacement at zero additional cost, without requiring the damaged item to be shipped back first.

## 4. Warranty & Quality Guarantee
- All electronics and accessories come with a **1-Year Limited Manufacturer Warranty** covering hardware and manufacturing defects.
- Accidental damage, water submersion, and normal wear and tear are not covered under the standard warranty. Extended 2-year accidental coverage is available at checkout.

## 5. Discounts, Promo Codes & Price Matching
- **First Order Discount:** New users get 15% off with promo code `WELCOME15`.
- **Student Discount:** Verified students receive a 10% year-round discount via StudentBeans.
- **Price Match Policy:** We match any competitor's listed price within 7 days of purchase if the item is in stock in the same color and size.

## 6. Customer Support Hours & Escalation
- **Live AI Support:** Available 24/7/365 through our website widget.
- **Human Support Team:** Available Monday through Saturday from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM EST.
- **Email Support:** support@trendystore.com (Average response time: 2 hours).
