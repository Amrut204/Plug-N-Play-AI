# Tuition and Hostel Fee Payment Policy

## Clause 2: Deadlines and Late Fine Schedule
1. The deadline for semester tuition fee settlement is the 15th of the commencement month of each academic term.
2. Grace Period & Surcharges:
   - A grace period of 5 calendar days is allowed without penalty.
   - For fees settled between the 21st day and 30th day, a daily fine of INR 100 is automatically compounded.
   - If dues remain unpaid after 30 days, hall tickets will be withheld and library circulation privileges suspended.
