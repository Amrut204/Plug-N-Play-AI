# Institute Merit & Need-Based Scholarship Guidelines

## Section A: Merit-Cum-Means Scholarship Criteria
To qualify for the annual institutional merit scholarship, students must satisfy all four of the following requirements simultaneously:
1. Academic Performance: Must maintain a Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) of 8.0 or above at the conclusion of the previous academic semester.
2. Attendance Discipline: Must have achieved an aggregate attendance of 80% or higher across all enrolled coursework.
3. Conduct and Backlogs: Must possess zero active backlogs or disciplinary reprimands on record.
4. Economic Means: Gross annual family income from all verifiable sources must not exceed INR 5,00,000 (INR 5 Lakhs).

Award Details:
Eligible scholars receive a 50% tuition fee waiver along with a book grant of INR 10,000 per academic year.
