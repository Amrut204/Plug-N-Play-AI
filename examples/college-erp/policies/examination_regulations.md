# Examination Regulations & Attendance Requirements

## Article 4: Attendance Mandate for End-Semester Examinations
1. Every candidate must secure a minimum attendance percentage of 75% in each individual enrolled subject to become eligible to appear for the End-Semester Examination.
2. Condonation of Shortage of Attendance:
   - Candidates having attendance between 65% and 74% in any subject may be granted condonation of attendance shortage on valid medical grounds, provided a genuine medical certificate and prescription are submitted within 7 days of absence.
   - A condonation processing fee of INR 1,500 per subject must be remitted prior to hall-ticket issuance.
3. Severe Shortage (Detention):
   - Candidates whose attendance falls below 65% under any circumstances are strictly not permitted to appear for the examinations. They shall be formally detained in the respective course and must re-register when the course is offered next academic year.
