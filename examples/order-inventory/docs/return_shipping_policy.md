# Return, Shipping & Replacement Policies

## 1. Shipping Options & Delivery Windows
- **Standard Ground**: 3–5 business days. Free on orders exceeding $75.
- **Expedited 2-Day**: 2 business days via FedEx/UPS. $14.99 flat rate.
- **Overnight Priority**: Next business day delivery if ordered before 14:00 EST. $29.99 flat rate.
- **Order Cut-Off**: Warehouse fulfillment dispatch occurs Monday through Friday at 16:00 EST.

## 2. Return Window & Eligibility
- **30-Day Money-Back Guarantee**: Items may be returned within 30 calendar days of confirmed carrier delivery date.
- **Condition Requirements**:
  - Products must be in original packaging with all cables, manuals, and accessories included.
  - Opened electronics are subject to a 10% restocking fee unless defective upon arrival.
  - Furniture items must be unassembled to qualify for full refund.
- **Non-Returnable Items**: Custom-built standing desks with personalized tabletop engravings, clearance/as-is items, and digital software keys.

## 3. Damaged or Defective Items (DOA Policy)
- Customers must report damaged or non-functional items within 48 hours of delivery.
- A free pre-paid return label is automatically issued upon photo verification.
- Replacement units are dispatched immediately via Expedited 2-Day shipping without waiting for the defective unit to reach the warehouse.

## 4. Tracking & Missing Packages
- If tracking shows "Delivered" but customer has not received the parcel, allow a 24-hour courier buffer window before filing a lost transit claim.
- VIP and Gold loyalty tier members receive immediate one-time complimentary re-dispatch for packages lost in transit.
